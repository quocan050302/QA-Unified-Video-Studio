One tiny gap could ruin a Roman shield wall.
At dawn, a rookie was still adjusting his grip.
The instructor spotted it,
and sent a wooden practice spear straight at the opening.
The rookie planted his foot, shoved his shield into place,
and the spear snapped aside.
Perfect drill. Almost.
His own spear was now tangled in his neighbor's strap.
The instructor didn't smile. But he nodded.
