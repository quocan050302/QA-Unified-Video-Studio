L01: At Rome's market hill rested a grain cart.
L02: Downhill, Baker Lucius stood beside his hot oven.
L03: A brake peg cracked; the cart rolled downhill.
L04: Market shoppers dodged street obstacles.
L05: Apprentice Titus sprinted; swiftly Titus caught the hemp rope.
L06: Titus whipped the rope around a bollard; Titus braced.
L07: The cart halted short; one loaf bounced off the tray.
L08: It arced high; it landed squarely on Centurion Varro's helmet.
L09: Nobody dared laugh; Centurion Varro kept walking.
L10: Behind him, the market cheered Titus.
