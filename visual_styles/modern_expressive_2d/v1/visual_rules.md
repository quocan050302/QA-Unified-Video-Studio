# Visual Rules — `modern_expressive_2d.v1`

## 1. Variable-Weight Calligraphic Ink Linework
1. **Outer Silhouette Contour**: Crisp dark espresso-ink (`#191210`), `0.95%–1.55%` of rendered character height (`3.0–9.5 px` in close-ups, `2.0–5.2 px` in wide shots), round caps/joins.
2. **Interior Creases & Features**: `38%–58%` of outer contour thickness with tapered endpoints for cloth folds, collarbone/jaw accents, hair locks, and finger separations.
3. **Depth-Separated Environment Ink**: Midground props use warm charcoal-umber (`#3A312B`); distant architecture uses atmospheric slate (`#545D6A`) so foreground characters read Effortlessly on mobile screens.

## 2. Character Proportions, Views & Silhouettes
1. **No Homogeneous Circle-Head Clones**: Characters use archetype-specific head-to-height ratios (`0.198–0.235`), varied jaw/cheek geometry, distinct hairstyles, and layered outfits with collars, belts, aprons, sashes, or coats.
2. **True Authored Multi-View Geometry**:
   - `front`: Symmetric cranium, paired ears, centered nose bridge, balanced shoulders.
   - `three_quarter`: Shifted cheek ogee contour, foreshortened far eye (`~0.78x`), nose bridge breaking inner plane, overlapping near shoulder.
   - `profile`: Side skull silhouette with projecting nose/lips/chin, profile almond eye, ear behind jaw hinge, side torso curve.
   - Scaling or flipping a front bust is **never** accepted as a profile or reverse angle.

## 3. Expressive Faces & Hands
1. Every speaking or reacting character supports at least **6 distinct expressions** (`neutral_attentive`, `wide_shock`, `strained_exertion`, `determined_focus`, `joyful_laugh`, `deadpan_disbelief`) plus speech visemes.
2. Hands use articulated palm + thumb + finger clusters (`open_reach`, `spread_shock`, `locked_grip`, `pinch_scroll`, `pointing`, `relaxed`) rather than featureless circles when interacting with props.

## 4. 2–3 Band Cel Shading & Motivated Rim Lighting
1. Every character and hero prop renders with **base local fill + sculptural core shadow band + motivated warm/cool key rim highlight**, plus a grounded contact shadow on the floor plane.
2. Palette saturation is vivid on focal subjects and controlled/harmonized across backgrounds.

## 5. Multi-Plane Environments & FX Discipline
1. Normal narrative scenes include 4 depth planes (`sky_backdrop`, `far_architecture`, `midground_stage`, `foreground_occluders`) with perspective floor/wall guides and tactile surface motifs.
2. Close-ups simplify background clutter (`TIER_1_GRAPHIC_MOOD_BACKDROP`) so facial acting and readable diegetic text dominate cleanly.
3. **FX Restraint**: Chromatic RGB fringes, heavy halftone screens, and neon bloom are **forbidden** on standard narrative shots. Motivated dust puffs, wind ribbons, and water droplets stay sparse and physically anchored.
